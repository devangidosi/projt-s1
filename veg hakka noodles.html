Veg Hakka Noodles

    ingredients
  • Hakka Noodles – 200 g
  • Oil – 2 tbsp
  • Garlic – 1 tbsp, finely chopped
  • Onion – 1, sliced
  • Carrot – 1, thinly sliced
  • Capsicum – 1, thinly sliced
  • Cabbage – 1 cup, shredded
  • Soy Sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Chilli Sauce – 1 tbs
  • Vinegar – 1 tsp
  • Black Pepper – ½ tsp
  • Salt – as needed
  • Spring Onion – for garnish

    • instruction

    1. Boil the Hakka noodles according to the package instructions. Drain and rinse them with cold water.
    2. Heat oil in a wok or large pan on high heat.
    3. Add chopped garlic and sauté for a few seconds.
    4. Add onion, carrot, capsicum, and cabbage. Stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes while keeping the vegetables slightly crunchy.
    5. Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and salt. Mix well.
    6. Add the boiled noodles and toss everything together gently.
    7. Cook for another 2–3 minutes until the noodles are well coated with the sauces.
    8. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
