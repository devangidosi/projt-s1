Lassi

    ingredients
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • ½ cup chilled milk
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp cardamom powder
  • 4–5 ice cubes
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts
  • Saffron for garnish (optional)

    • instruction

    1. Add yogurt, chilled milk, sugar, cardamom powder, and ice cubes to a blender.
    2. Blend for 30–40 seconds until smooth and creamy.
    3. Taste and adjust the sweetness if needed.
    4. Pour the lassi into a chilled glass.
    5. Garnish with chopped nuts and a few saffron strands.
    6. Serve immediately and enjoy chilled.
    ❤ ________

    Lassi is a refreshing and creamy Indian yogurt-based drink loved for its smooth and cooling taste. Made with fresh yogurt and a touch of sweetness, it is perfect for warm days. Its creamy texture and refreshing flavor make it a delightful drink. It can be enjoyed plain or topped with a little cream and nuts.
