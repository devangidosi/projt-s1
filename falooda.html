Falooda

    ingredients
  • 2 cups chilled milk
  • 2 tbsp rose syrup
  • 2 tbsp falooda sev
  • 1 tbsp basil seeds (sabja)
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts
  • Jelly, as needed
  • Ice cubes, as needed

    • instruction

    1. Soak the basil seeds in water for 10–15 minutes, then drain them.
    2. Boil the falooda sev according to the package instructions and let it cool.
    3. Mix chilled milk with sugar and rose syrup.
    4. In a glass, add basil seeds, falooda sev, and jelly in layers.
    5. Pour the chilled milk mixture over the layers.
    6. Top with a scoop of ice cream and chopped nuts.
    7. Drizzle some rose syrup on top and serve chilled.
    ❤ ________

    Falooda is a delicious and refreshing Indian dessert drink loved for its colorful layers and creamy texture. Made with chilled milk, sweet syrup, falooda sev, and basil seeds, it offers a delightful mix of flavors. Topped with ice cream and nuts, it looks as tempting as it tastes. It is a perfect treat for hot summer days.
