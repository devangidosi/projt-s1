Veg Manchurian

    ingredients
  • Cabbage – 1 cup, finely chopped
  • Carrot – ½ cup, grated
  • Capsicum – ¼ cup, finely chopped
  • Maida – 3 tbsp
  • Corn flour – 3 tbsp
  • Garlic – 1 tsp, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Red chilli sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Tomato ketchup – 1 tbsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Oil – for frying and cooking
  • Spring onions – for garnish
  • Water – as needed

    • instruction

    1. Mix cabbage, carrot, capsicum, 2 tbsp corn flour, maida, and salt in a bowl. Add a little water and make a soft mixture.
    2. Shape the mixture into small balls and fry them in hot oil until golden and crispy.
    3. Heat a little oil in a pan and sauté the chopped garlic.
    4. Add soy sauce, chilli sauce, tomato ketchup, and a little water. Mix well.
    5. Mix 1 tbsp corn flour with some water and add it to the pan. Cook until the sauce becomes slightly thick.
    6. Add the fried Manchurian balls and gently mix until they are well coated with the sauce.
    7. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.
