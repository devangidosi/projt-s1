Shrikhand

    ingredients
  • 2 cups thick yogurt (hung curd)
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder
  • 4–5 saffron strands
  • 2 tbsp chopped almonds and pistachios

    • instruction

    1. Strain the yogurt well to remove excess water.
    2. Transfer the thick yogurt to a bowl.
    3. Add powdered sugar and mix until smooth.
    4. Add cardamom powder and saffron.
    5. Mix everything well until creamy and smooth.
    6. Garnish with chopped nuts and chill before serving.
    ❤ ________

    Shrikhand is a creamy and refreshing Indian dessert made from thickened yogurt and delicately flavored with sugar and cardamom. Its smooth texture and gentle sweetness make it a delightful treat, especially when served chilled.
