Gulab jamum

    ingredients
  • 1 cup khoya (mawa)
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp milk
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 2–3 cardamom pods
  • Oil or ghee for frying

    • instruction

    1. Mix khoya, flour, and milk to make a soft dough.
    2. Make small, smooth balls from the dough.
    3. Heat oil or ghee and fry the balls on low-medium heat until golden brown.
    4. Boil sugar and water together to prepare the syrup.
    5. Add cardamom for a light fragrance.
    6. Soak the fried gulab jamuns in warm sugar syrup for 1–2 hours.
    7. Serve warm or chilled.
    ❤ ________

    Gulab Jamun is a beloved Indian sweet known for its soft, melt-in-the-mouth texture and rich, syrupy sweetness. Golden-brown balls are gently soaked in fragrant sugar syrup, making every bite delicious and comforting. It is a classic dessert enjoyed on festivals, celebrations, and special occasions.
