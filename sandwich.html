Cheese Sandwich

    ingredients
  • 4 bread slices
  • 1 small tomato, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced
  • 2 cheese slices
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp green chutney

    • instruction

    1. Bread slices par butter aur green chutney lagayein.
    2. Tomato, onion aur cucumber ke slices arrange karein.
    3. Cheese slices add karein aur salt aur black pepper sprinkle karein.
    4. Upar se mayonnaise lagakar doosri bread slice se cover karein.
    5. Sandwich ko grill ya toast karein jab tak bread golden and crispy na ho jaye.
    6. Sandwich ko cut karke fresh aur warm serve karein.
