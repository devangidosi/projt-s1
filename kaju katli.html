Kaju Katli

    ingredients
  • 1 cup cashews
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tsp ghee
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder

    • instruction

    1. Grind the cashews into a fine powder.
    2. Prepare a light sugar syrup with sugar and water.
    3. Add cashew powder and cardamom powder to the syrup.
    4. Cook on low heat while stirring until it forms a soft dough.
    5. Add a little ghee and knead the dough until smooth.
    6. Roll it evenly between butter paper sheets.
    7. Cut into diamond shapes and serve.
    ❤ ________

    Kaju Katli is one of the most cherished Indian sweets, known for its elegant appearance, rich cashew flavor, and silky-smooth texture. Made with finely ground cashews and sugar, this delightful sweet is gently prepared into a soft dough and traditionally cut into delicate diamond-shaped pieces. Its subtle sweetness and luxurious taste make it a popular choice for festivals, celebrations, gifting, or simply enjoying a special dessert at home.
