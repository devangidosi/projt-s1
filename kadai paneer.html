kadai Paneer

    ingredients
  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1–2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander for garnishing

    • instruction

    1. Heat oil in a kadai and add cumin seeds. Let them crackle.
    2. Add chopped onions and sauté until they become light golden.
    3. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Cook for 1–2 minutes.
    4. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric, red chilli powder, and coriander powder. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
    5. Add chopped capsicum and cook for 2–3 minutes while keeping it slightly crunchy.
    6. Add paneer cubes, salt, and garam masala. Mix gently.
    7. Sprinkle kasuri methi and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
    8. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with naan, roti, or rice.

    Kadai Paneer is a delicious and flavorful North Indian dish made with soft paneer, colorful bell peppers, onions, and a rich blend of aromatic spices. Cooked in a traditional kadai, this dish has a perfect combination of spicy, tangy, and mildly creamy flavors. Its vibrant texture and irresistible aroma make it a popular choice for both everyday meals and special occasions. Kadai Paneer tastes best when served hot with naan, roti, or fragrant basmati rice.
