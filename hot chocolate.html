Hot Chocolate

    ingredients
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 50 g dark or milk chocolate, chopped
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • Whipped cream for topping (optional)
  • Chocolate shavings for garnish (optional)

    • instruction

    1. Heat the milk in a saucepan over medium heat without boiling it.
    2. Add cocoa powder and sugar, then whisk until smooth.
    3. Add the chopped chocolate and stir until it completely melts.
    4. Mix in the vanilla essence and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
    5. Pour the hot chocolate into a mug.
    6. Top with whipped cream and chocolate shavings if desired.
    7. Serve warm and enjoy!
    ❤ ________

    Hot Chocolate is a warm, creamy, and comforting drink made with rich chocolate and smooth milk. Its velvety texture and delicious chocolate flavor make every sip special. Topped with whipped cream or chocolate shavings, it looks even more tempting. It is a perfect drink for cozy evenings and chilly days.
