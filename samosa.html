Samosa

    ingredients
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 3–4 potatoes, boiled
  • ½ cup green peas
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying

    • instruction

    1. Prepare the dough: Mix maida, salt and oil. Add water gradually and knead into a firm dough. Rest it for 15–20 minutes.
    2. Prepare the filling: Heat a little oil and add cumin seeds, ginger and green chili. Add boiled potatoes and peas.
    3. Add salt, red chili powder, coriander powder and garam masala. Mix well and cook for a few minutes. Let the filling cool.
    4. Divide the dough into small balls and roll each one into a thin oval shape.
    5. Cut the oval in half, make a cone and fill it with the prepared potato mixture.
    6. Seal the edges properly with a little water.
    7. Heat oil and fry the samosas on medium heat until they become golden, crisp and delicious.
    8. Serve hot with mint chutney or tamarind chutney.

    Samosa is a beloved Indian snack known for its crispy golden crust and deliciously flavorful filling. Traditionally filled with a savory mixture of spiced potatoes and peas, it offers a perfect balance of crunchy texture and rich, aromatic flavors. Served hot with tangy chutney, samosa makes a delightful tea-time snack and a popular choice for gatherings, celebrations, and everyday cravings.
