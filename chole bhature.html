Chole Bhature

ingredients

  • 2 cups Chickpeas (Chole)
  • 1 Onion, finely chopped
  • 2 Tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1–2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tsp Chole Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • Fresh Coriander

instruction

  1. Soak the chickpeas overnight and boil them until soft
  2. Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds, and sauté the chopped onion until golden
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes, then cook until soft

Chole Bhature is a beloved North Indian classic that brings together spicy, flavorful chickpea curry and soft, fluffy, golden-brown bhature. The chole is cooked with aromatic spices, tomatoes, and herbs to create a rich and hearty gravy, while the freshly fried bhature add a light and satisfying texture. Traditionally served with onions, tangy pickle, and refreshing chutney, Chole Bhature is a delicious and indulgent meal that captures the authentic flavors of Indian cuisine.
