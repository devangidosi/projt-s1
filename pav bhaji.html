Pav Bhaji

ingredients

  • 4–5 Pav (Dinner Rolls)
  • 2 Medium Potatoes, boiled
  • 1 Cup Cauliflower, chopped
  • ½ Cup Green Peas
  • 1 Medium Capsicum, finely chopped
  • 2 Medium Tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 Large Onion, finely chopped
  • 1–2 Green Chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 Teaspoon Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 2 Tablespoons Butter
  • 1–2 Tablespoons Pav Bhaji Masala
  • ½ Teaspoon Red Chili Powder
  • ½ Teaspoon Turmeric Powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • ½ Cup Water, as needed
  • 1 Tablespoon Fresh Coriander, chopped
  • 1 Tablespoon Lemon Juice
  • Extra Butter for serving
  • Chopped Onion and Lemon Wedges for garnish

instruction

  1. Boil the potatoes and mixed vegetables until sof
  2. Heat butter in a pan and sauté the chopped onion and capsicum.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  4. Add Pav Bhaji Masala, chili powder, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Add the boiled vegetables and mash everything together
  6. Add a little water and cook for 5–7 minutes
  7. Add lemon juice and fresh coriander.
  8. Toast the pav with butter and serve hot with the bhaji.

Pav Bhaji is a popular and flavorful Indian street food loved for its rich taste and comforting texture. This delicious dish combines a spicy, buttery blend of mashed vegetables cooked with aromatic spices and served with soft, toasted pav. Topped with fresh coriander, chopped onions, and a touch of butter, Pav Bhaji offers a perfect balance of tangy, spicy, and savory flavors. It is an easy and satisfying meal that brings the authentic taste of Indian street food right to your table.
