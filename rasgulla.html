Rasgulla

    ingredients
  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • 2–3 drops rose water
  • 1 tsp semolina (optional)

    • instruction

    1. Boil the milk and add lemon juice to separate the chhena.
    2. Strain the chhena and remove the extra water.
    3. Knead the chhena until smooth and soft.
    4. Make small, smooth balls from the chhena.
    5. Boil sugar and water together to prepare the syrup.
    6. Add the rasgulla balls to the syrup and cook for 12–15 minutes.
    7. Let them cool slightly and serve fresh or chilled.
    ❤ ________

    Rasgulla is a popular Indian sweet known for its soft, spongy texture and juicy sweetness. Made from fresh chhena and gently cooked in a light sugar syrup, each rasgulla is delicate, refreshing, and full of flavor. Its simple yet delightful taste makes it a perfect dessert for festivals, celebrations, or whenever you are craving something sweet.
