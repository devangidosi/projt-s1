Momos

    ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 cup finely chopped cabbage
  • ½ cup finely chopped carrot
  • ½ cup finely chopped capsicum
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped spring onion
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Water as needed

    • instruction

    1. Prepare the Dough: Mix maida and a pinch of salt. Add water gradually and knead into a smooth, soft dough. Cover and rest for 15–20 minutes.
    2. Prepare the Filling: Heat a little oil and sauté ginger-garlic paste. Add the chopped vegetables, soy sauce, black pepper, and salt. Cook for a few minutes until slightly tender
    3. Shape the Momos: Divide the dough into small portions and roll each into a thin circle. Place a small amount of filling in the center and fold the edges to seal the momo.
    4. Steam: Arrange the momos in a greased steamer basket, leaving some space between them. Steam for about 10–12 minutes until the wrappers become soft and slightly glossy.
    5. Serve: Serve the hot momos immediately with your favorite spicy momo chutney.

    Momos are a delightful and comforting treat known for their soft, delicate texture and irresistible taste. These perfectly shaped dumplings are enjoyed warm and are loved for their simple yet satisfying character. Paired with a flavorful chutney, momos create a delicious combination that makes every bite enjoyable. Whether served as a quick snack, a casual meal, or a favorite street-food delight, momos have a special way of bringing comfort and flavor to the table.
