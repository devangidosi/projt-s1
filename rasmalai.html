Rasmalai

    ingredients
  • 1 litre full-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 4–5 saffron strands
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped almonds and pistachios

    • instruction

    1. Boil the milk and add lemon juice to make chhena.
    2. Strain the chhena and remove the extra water.
    3. Knead it well and make small, smooth discs.
    4. Boil sugar and water together to prepare the syrup.
    5. Add the chhena discs and cook for 10–12 minutes.
    6. In another pan, boil milk until slightly thick and add sugar, saffron, and cardamom.
    7. Add the cooked discs to the flavored milk and chill before serving.
    8. Garnish with chopped nuts and enjoy.
    ❤ ________

    Rasmalai is a rich and creamy Indian dessert made with soft, spongy cheese dumplings soaked in sweet, saffron-flavored milk. Its delicate texture and luxurious taste make it a favorite for festive occasions. Served chilled with a garnish of nuts, it is both refreshing and indulgent.
