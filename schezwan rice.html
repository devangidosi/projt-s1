Schezwan rice

    ingredients
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, capsicum, cabbage)
  • 2 tbsp Schezwan sauce
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped garlic
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Spring onions for garnish

    • instruction

    1. Heat oil in a wok or large pan and sauté the chopped garlic and onion until fragrant.
    2. Add the mixed vegetables and stir-fry on high heat for 2–3 minutes.
    3. Add Schezwan sauce, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
    4. Add the cooked rice and gently toss everything together until well combined.
    5. Cook for another 2–3 minutes on high heat, stirring occasionally.
    6. Garnish with spring onions and serve hot.

    Schezwan Rice is a delicious and flavorful Indo-Chinese dish made with fragrant rice, colorful vegetables, and a bold Schezwan sauce. Tossed together with aromatic spices, garlic, and a touch of soy sauce, this rice offers the perfect balance of spicy, savory, and smoky flavors. Quick to prepare and incredibly satisfying, Schezwan Rice is an ideal choice for a flavorful lunch, dinner, or a delightful side dish. Its vibrant appearance and rich taste make it a favorite among Indo-Chinese food lovers.
