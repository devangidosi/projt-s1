Vada pav

    ingredients
  • 4 pav
  • 3–4 potatoes, boiled
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger, grated
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying
  • Green chutney
  • Tamarind chutney
  • Dry garlic chutney

    • instruction

    1. Prepare the filling: Mash the boiled potatoes and mix with green chili, ginger, mustard seeds, turmeric, red chili powder, and salt.
    2. Make small round balls from the potato mixture.
    3. Prepare the batter: Mix besan, salt, and a little water to make a smooth, medium-thick batter.
    4. Dip each potato ball into the batter and coat it well
    5. Deep-fry the coated potato balls until golden and crispy.
    6. Cut the pav from the middle and spread green chutney, tamarind chutney, and garlic chutney.
    7. Place the hot vada inside the pav and serve fresh and warm.

    Vada Pav is a popular Indian street food known for its crispy potato vada, soft pav, and flavorful chutneys. The combination of spicy green chutney, tangy tamarind chutney, and aromatic garlic chutney makes every bite delicious and satisfying. Perfect as a quick snack or evening treat, Vada Pav is a simple yet flavorful favorite loved by people of all ages.
