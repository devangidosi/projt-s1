Paneer tikka

ingredients

  • 250g panner
  • 1 cup curd
  • 1 capsicum
  • 1 onion
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon ginger galic paste
  • salt to taste

instruction

  1. cut paneer,onion and capsicum into pieces
  2. mix curd and spices in a bowl
  3. add paneer and vegetables and coat well
  4. cook until the paneer is golden and ready

Paneer Tikka is a classic Indian delicacy loved for its smoky aroma, rich flavors, and perfectly grilled texture. Made with soft, creamy paneer marinated in a blend of aromatic spices, yogurt, and fresh herbs, each piece is grilled to perfection until beautifully charred on the outside while remaining tender and juicy inside. Served with refreshing mint chutney, onion rings, and a touch of lemon, Paneer Tikka makes a delicious appetizer and an elegant addition to any meal. Its irresistible combination of spices and smoky flavors makes it a favorite for celebrations, gatherings, and everyday cravings.
