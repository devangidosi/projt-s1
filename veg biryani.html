Veg Biryani

ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 1 cup Mixed Vegetables (carrot, beans, peas)
  • 1 Onion, sliced
  • 1 Tomato, chopped
  • 1/2 cup Curd
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ginger-Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Biryani Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh Coriander
  • 3–4 cups Water

instruction

  1. Wash and soak the rice for about 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the sliced onions until golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and chopped tomatoes. Cook until soft.
  4. Add the mixed vegetables, turmeric, red chilli powder, biryani masala, and salt. Mix well.
  5. Add curd and cook the vegetables for a few minutes.
  6. Add the soaked rice and gently mix everything together.
  7. Pour in the required water and bring it to a boil.
  8. Cover the pan and cook on low heat until the rice is soft and the water is absorbed
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and gently fluff the rice.
  10. Serve hot with raita or your favorite side dish
