Dahi puri

    ingredients
  • 12–15 puris
  • 1 boiled potato, mashed
  • ½ cup boiled chickpeas
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp green chutney
  • 2 tbsp tamarind chutney
  • ½ cup sev
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp chaat masala
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • Fresh coriander
  • Salt to taste

    • instruction

    1. Prepare the puris by making a small hole in the center of each puri.
    2. Fill each puri with boiled potatoes and cooked chickpeas.
    3. Add a little chopped onion and tomato for freshness.
    4. Drizzle green chutney and sweet tamarind chutney over each puri.
    5. Add whisked curd and sprinkle chaat masala and roasted cumin powder.
    6. Garnish with sev, fresh coriander, and a little red chilli powder.
    7. Serve the Dahi Puri immediately for the best crispy and delicious taste.

    Dahi Puri is a delicious and refreshing Indian chaat that combines crispy puris with creamy yogurt and flavorful chutneys. Topped with tangy tamarind chutney, spicy green chutney, crunchy sev, and aromatic spices, every bite offers a delightful mix of sweet, spicy, tangy, and creamy flavors. Perfect as a light snack or evening treat, Dahi Puri is a colorful and irresistible favorite for chaat lovers.
