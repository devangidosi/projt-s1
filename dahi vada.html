Dahi vada

ingredients

  • 1 cup Urad Dal
  • 2 cups Curd (Dahi)
  • 1 Green Chilli
  • 1 tsp Ginger
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp Cumin Seeds
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Roasted Cumin Powder
  • Tamarind Chutney
  • Green Chutney
  • Fresh Coriander

instruction

  1. Soak the urad dal in water for 4–5 hours.
  2. Drain the water and grind the dal into a smooth, thick batter.
  3. Add ginger, green chilli, cumin seeds, and salt. Mix well.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and fry small portions of the batter until golden brown.
  5. Put the fried vadas in warm water for about 10 minutes.
  6. Gently press the vadas to remove extra water.
  7. Whisk the curd until smooth and add a little salt and sugar.
  8. Place the vadas in a serving bowl and pour the curd over them.
  9. Add green chutney, tamarind chutney, red chilli powder, and roasted cumin powder.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve chilled.
❤__________ Dahi Vada is a popular Indian delicacy loved for its soft texture, creamy curd, and delicious blend of sweet and tangy flavors. Made with soft lentil dumplings soaked in smooth, chilled yogurt, this delightful dish is topped with flavorful tamarind chutney, green chutney, and aromatic spices. Every bite offers a perfect combination of creamy, tangy, and mildly spicy flavors, making Dahi Vada an irresistible appetizer or refreshing snack.
