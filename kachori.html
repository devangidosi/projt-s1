Kachori

    ingredients
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 cup moong dal
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp fennel seeds
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying

    • instruction

    1. Mix maida, salt, and oil in a bowl. Add water gradually and make a soft dough. Let it rest for 15–20 minutes.
    2. Soak the moong dal for a few hours, then drain and grind it coarsely.
    3. Heat a little oil and add cumin and fennel seeds. Add the ground dal and spices.
    4. Cook the filling until it becomes dry and aromatic. Let it cool.
    5. Divide the dough into small balls and flatten each one.
    6. Place some filling in the center, seal the edges, and gently roll into a small kachori.
    7. Heat oil on medium-low heat and fry the kachoris until golden and crispy.
    8. Serve hot with tamarind chutney or green chutney.
    ❤ ________

    Kachori is a popular Indian snack loved for its crispy, flaky crust and flavorful spiced filling. Golden and crunchy on the outside, it is filled with a delicious blend of aromatic spices that makes every bite satisfying. Served with tangy chutney, Kachori is a perfect choice for a tasty tea-time or evening snack.
