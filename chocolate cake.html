Chocolate cake

    ingredients
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • ¾ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

    • instruction

    1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a cake pan with oil or butter.
    2. In a bowl, mix flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
    3. In another bowl, whisk eggs, sugar, milk, oil, and vanilla essence until smooth.
    4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix gently.
    5. Add hot water and mix until you get a smooth cake batter.
    6. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan and bake for 30–35 minutes.
    7. Check with a toothpick; if it comes out clean, the cake is ready.
    8. Let the cake cool completely before decorating or serving.
