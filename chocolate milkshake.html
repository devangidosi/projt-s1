Chocolate Milkshake

    ingredients
  • 2 cups chilled milk
  • 2 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • Ice cubes, as needed
  • Chocolate chips or grated chocolate for topping

    • instruction

    1. Blender mein chilled milk, chocolate syrup, sugar, cocoa powder aur chocolate ice cream daalein.
    2. Sabhi ingredients ko 30–40 seconds tak smooth aur creamy hone tak blend karein
    3. Zarurat ho to ice cubes daal kar ek baar phir blend karein.
    4. Milkshake ko glass mein pour karein.
    5. Upar chocolate syrup aur grated chocolate se garnish karke chilled serve karein.
    ❤ ________

    Chocolate Milkshake is a rich, creamy, and refreshing drink loved by chocolate lovers of all ages. Made with smooth chocolate and chilled milk, it has a deliciously thick texture and a sweet, indulgent flavor. It is perfect for a quick treat or a refreshing drink on a warm day.
