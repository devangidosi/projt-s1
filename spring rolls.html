Spring Rolls

    ingredients
  • 8–10 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 cup finely shredded cabbage
  • ½ cup grated carrot
  • ½ cup finely chopped capsicum
  • ¼ cup spring onion
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying

    • instruction

    1. Prepare the Filling: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté briefly. Add cabbage, carrot, capsicum, and spring onion. Stir-fry on high heat for a few minutes.
    2. Season: Add soy sauce, black pepper, and salt. Mix well and cook until the vegetables are slightly tender but still crunchy. Allow the filling to cool.
    3. Prepare the Seal: Mix all-purpose flour with a little water to make a smooth, thick paste.
    4. Fill & Roll: Place a spring roll wrapper on a flat surface. Add a small amount of filling near one edge. Fold the sides inward and roll it tightly. Seal the edge with the flour paste
    5. Fry: Heat oil and carefully fry the spring rolls until they turn crisp and golden.
    6. Serve: Drain on a paper towel and serve hot with your favorite dipping sauce.
