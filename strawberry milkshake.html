Strawberry Milkshake

    ingredients
  • 1 cup fresh strawberries
  • 1½ cups chilled milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • 4–5 ice cubes
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence (optional)
  • Whipped cream for topping (optional)

    • instruction

    1. Wash and remove the green tops from the strawberries.
    2. Add strawberries, chilled milk, sugar, ice cream, and ice cubes to a blender.
    3. Blend everything until smooth and creamy.
    4. Taste and add a little more sugar if needed.
    5. Pour the milkshake into a chilled glass.
    6. Top with whipped cream and a fresh strawberry if desired.
    7. Serve immediately and enjoy chilled!

    Strawberry Milkshake is a creamy and refreshing drink made with fresh strawberries and chilled milk. Its sweet fruity flavor and smooth texture make it a delicious treat for all ages. Served cold with a little whipped cream, it looks as delightful as it tastes. It is a perfect choice for a refreshing summer drink.
