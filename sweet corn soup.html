Sweet Corn Soup

    ingredients
  • 1 cup sweet corn
  • ½ cup finely chopped carrots
  • ¼ cup finely chopped spring onion
  • 3 cups vegetable stock or water
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp oil

    • instruction

    1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the ginger-garlic paste for a few seconds.
    2. Add sweet corn and chopped vegetables. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes.
    3. Add vegetable stock or water and bring it to a gentle boil.
    4. Mix cornflour with a little water to make a smooth slurry, then add it to the soup while stirring continuously.
    5. Add soy sauce, black pepper, and salt. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until the soup becomes slightly thick.
    6. Garnish with spring onion and serve the soup hot and fresh.
