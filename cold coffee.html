Cold Coffee

    ingredients
  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 1½ tsp instant coffee
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 scoops vanilla or coffee ice cream
  • 4–5 ice cubes
  • 1 tbsp chocolate syrup
  • Whipped cream for topping (optional)

    • instruction

    1. Add chilled milk, coffee, sugar, ice cream, chocolate syrup, and ice cubes to a blender.
    2. Blend everything for 30–40 seconds until smooth and frothy.
    3. Pour the cold coffee into a chilled glass.
    4. Top with whipped cream and a little chocolate syrup if desired.
    5. Serve immediately and enjoy chilled.
    ❤ ________

    Cold Coffee is a refreshing and creamy drink made with chilled milk and rich coffee. Its smooth texture and delicious coffee flavor make every sip enjoyable. Served cold with ice, it is a perfect beverage for warm days. A little chocolate syrup can make it even more tempting.
