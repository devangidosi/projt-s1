Dhokla

    ingredients
  • 1 cup gram flour (besan)
  • ½ cup yogurt
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp Eno fruit salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 5–6 curry leaves
  • 1–2 green chilies
  • Fresh coriander

    • instruction

    1. In a bowl, mix gram flour, yogurt, water, turmeric, sugar, lemon juice, and salt to make a smooth batter.
    2. Let the batter rest for 10–15 minutes.
    3. Add Eno fruit salt just before steaming and gently mix.
    4. Grease a steaming tray with oil and pour the batter into it.
    5. Steam the dhokla for 15–20 minutes until soft and fluffy.
    6. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds, curry leaves, and green chilies.
    7. Pour the tempering over the steamed dhokla and garnish with fresh coriander.
    8. Cut into pieces and serve fresh with green chutney.

    Dhokla is a delightful Gujarati specialty that turns simple ingredients into a soft, airy, and flavorful treat. Its delicate texture and mild savory taste make every bite light yet satisfying. Finished with a tempting tempering and served with fresh green chutney, Dhokla brings a refreshing touch to any snack table. It is a perfect blend of simplicity, tradition, and irresistible flavor.
