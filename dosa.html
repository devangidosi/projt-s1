Dosa

ingredients

    2 cups Rice

  • 1 cup Urad Dal
  • 1/2 tsp Fenugreek Seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for cooking

instruction

  1. Wash and soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately for 4–6 hours.
  2. Drain the water and grind them together into a smooth batter
  3. Add some water if needed and mix well.
  4. Cover the batter and let it ferment overnight or for 8–10 hours
  5. Add salt and mix the fermented batter gently.
  6. Heat a non-stick pan or tawa and lightly grease it with oil.
  7. Pour a ladleful of batter onto the pan and spread it into a thin, round dosa.
  8. Drizzle a little oil around the edges and cook until the dosa becomes golden and crispy.
  9. Fold the dosa and serve it hot.
  10. Serve with coconut chutney and sambar for a delicious traditional meal.

Dosa is a classic South Indian delicacy known for its crisp texture, delicate flavor, and irresistible aroma. Made from a fermented blend of rice and lentils, this golden-brown dish is traditionally served hot with flavorful coconut chutney and comforting sambar. Light yet satisfying, Dosa is a versatile favorite that makes a perfect breakfast, snack, or wholesome meal. Its simple ingredients and authentic taste make it a timeless dish loved across India and beyond.
